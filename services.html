Custom Luxury Kitchens

Complete kitchens designed from a blank page. We compose layout, lighting, stone, hardware and cabinetry as a single piece — so the room feels inevitable, not assembled.

  • Full-space design from first sketch
  • Custom layouts for how you cook & gather
  • Stone, lighting & hardware curation
  • White-glove installation & finishing
02

Bespoke Cabinetry

Cabinetry is our mother tongue. Vanities, pantries, wall units and storage walls — built to the millimetre in premium woods and flawless finishes, for kitchens and beyond.

  • Made-to-measure — no stock sizes
  • Premium woods, lacquers & veneers
  • Brass, bronze & custom hardware
  • Pantries, vanities, wall units & more
03

Kitchen Renovations

Transformations of existing spaces. We reimagine the layout, replace the cabinetry, stone and fixtures — and manage every trade start to finish, with obsessive site care.

  • Layout reconfiguration & space planning
  • Full cabinetry replacement
  • Stone, backsplash & fixture upgrades
  • Single point of contact throughout

Commercial & Residential

From family homes to restaurants, offices and retail — millwork and kitchens engineered for spaces that work as hard as they look.

  • Restaurant & hospitality kitchens
  • Office & retail millwork
  • Multi-unit residential projects
  • Built to commercial standards
