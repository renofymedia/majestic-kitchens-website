Our story

A studio built on obsession.

Majestic Kitchens began with a simple frustration: kitchens that looked expensive but felt generic. So we built a studio that does the opposite — every project designed from a blank page, every cabinet made for its exact space.

We are designers and craftsmen under one roof. The person who draws your kitchen is in conversation with the person who builds it — so nothing is lost between the drawing and the doing.

Three years in, our clients have given us 21 five-star Google reviews — and a reputation across Brampton and the GTA for kitchens that feel inevitable.

What we believe

Three principles, zero shortcuts.

I.

Built to the millimetre

Stock sizes are a compromise. We measure, we build, we fit — cabinetry that meets your walls like it grew there.

II.

One team, start to finish

Design, build and installation by the same dedicated crew. One point of contact, total accountability.

III.

Materials with soul

Veined stone, honest timber, brushed brass — materials chosen to be beautiful on day one and day one thousand.

How it unfolds

From first conversation to final reveal.

01

Consultation

We visit, listen and measure. Your routines, your taste, your space — the brief everything else is built on.

02

Design

Detailed drawings and material selections — stone, wood, brass, light — refined with you until it feels inevitable.

03

Craft

Your cabinetry is built to order and installed by our own craftsmen, with obsessive site care throughout.

04

Reveal

Final polish, walkthrough and handover. The moment your kitchen stops being a project and starts being yours.
